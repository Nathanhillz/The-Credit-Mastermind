About

I learned this
the hard way.

I didn't grow up with financial education. I learned everything the hard way — fixing my own credit while raising my kids and building my life from scratch.

For the past seven years I've dedicated myself to teaching people the power of good credit. Not on paper — in real life. Families who never thought they'd qualify walking into their dream home. Single moms getting their keys. Entrepreneurs building businesses using credit the right way.

Then I got the technical grounding to match: a Bachelor's in Accounting from the University of Illinois, and time as an accountant at both PwC and Deloitte. Today I hold my CPA, CFA and CIMA — which means credit, tax, retirement and investments aren't four vendors I coordinate between. They're one conversation with one person who can see the whole picture.

Credit Mastermind is that conversation. We're not just disputing — we're educating, empowering, and creating generational change.
