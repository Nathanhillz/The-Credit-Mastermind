What we do

Four disciplines,
one advisor.

Credit was never the whole story — it's the door most people walk through first. What comes next is tax, retirement, and the portfolio itself. You shouldn't need four different advisors to get there.

01

Credit Repair & Education

Where it all started. We don't just dispute — we educate, so you understand the system instead of fearing it.

Every engagement opens with all three bureaus and a plain-English read of what is actually holding the file back — then a documented plan to clear it, in the order lenders expect to see it cleared.

  • Three-bureau report review
  • Dispute strategy & documentation
  • Collections & charge-off removal
  • Utilisation & score coaching
  • Home-buying & funding readiness
  • Credit education for the whole household
02

Tax Planning & Preparation

Filing is the last five minutes. The other eleven months are where the money is actually saved.

Individuals and small businesses, worked on through the year rather than rushed in April — so the return is a formality instead of a surprise, and an IRS notice gets answered once, properly.

  • Individual & small business returns
  • Year-round tax planning
  • Deduction & credit optimization
  • Quarterly estimates for the self-employed
  • IRS notice support & correspondence
  • Record-keeping that survives a review
03

Retirement — 401(k) & IRA

The account your employer hands you and the one you open yourself should be working as a single strategy.

Most people have both and have never had them looked at together. We map contributions across every plan you hold, test the rollover question against real fund costs, and only move money for a reason.

  • Contribution & match strategy
  • Traditional vs. Roth IRA
  • Rollovers done for the right reason
  • Plan fund-cost and fee review
  • Retirement projections
  • Beneficiary and allocation checks
04

Stocks & Asset Management

Portfolio management built around what the money is actually for — not a risk questionnaire and a template.

Allocation, fund selection and rebalancing set against your real timeline and tax position, then reviewed on a discipline rather than on whatever the market did that week.

  • Portfolio construction
  • Stocks, funds & allocation
  • Rebalancing discipline
  • Tax-aware investing
  • Consolidation across scattered accounts
  • Ongoing review and client reporting
